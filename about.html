THE CHANNEL

Started for fun. Built into something real.

I'm Shaun — Swerve256 on stream. I play RPGs, Soulslikes, challenge runs, flight sims and whatever else gives us something worth talking about. The channel isn't about pretending every attempt is perfect. It's about figuring it out live, laughing when it goes wrong and locking in when it matters.

After years of grinding, the channel crossed 1,000 subscribers. That milestone isn't the finish line. It's proof that a small community can turn into something worth building around.
